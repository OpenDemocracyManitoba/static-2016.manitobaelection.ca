Charleswood

This electoral division is located at the western tip of Winnipeg, south of the Assiniboine River. The population of the Charleswood electoral division in 2008 was 20,100. The most recent MLA to represent this area was Myrna Driedger (Progressive Conservative Party) between 1998 and 2016.

The voter turnout for the Charleswood electoral division during the 2011 provincial election was 61%.
