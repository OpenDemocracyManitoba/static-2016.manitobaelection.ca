Fort Garry-Riverview

This electoral division is located in the south central portion of Winnipeg, extending south of Osborne Junction along the west side of the Red River to just north of Bishop Grandin Boulevard. The population of Fort Garry - Riverview in 2008 was 20,400. The most recent MLA to represent this area was James Allum (New Democratic Party) between 2011 and 2016.

The voter turnout for Fort Garry - Riverview during the 2011 provincial election was 65%.
