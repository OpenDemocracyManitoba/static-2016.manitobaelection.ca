Thompson

This electoral division is located in the northern portion of Manitoba, north of Lake Winnipeg. The population of the Thompson electoral division in 2008 was 18,900. The most recent MLA to represent this area was Steve Ashton (New Democratic Party) between 1981 and 2016.

The voter turnout for Thompson (electoral division) during the 2011 provincial election was 37%.
